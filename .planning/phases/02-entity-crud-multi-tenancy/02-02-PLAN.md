## Phase Goal

**As an** authenticated user, **I want to** create, view, update, and soft-delete my own houses via REST, **so that** I manage the top of my home hierarchy and no other user can reach my houses.

<objective>
Deliver the full House CRUD vertical slice end-to-end: TypeBox-validated routes → ownership-embedded service → cascade soft-delete → multi-tenancy (404-not-403) and auth (401) enforcement, with the DB-touching test suite. This is the reference slice the Room and Device slices mirror.

Purpose: Houses are the root of the ownership hierarchy; every room and device is scoped under a house. Getting the ownership query pattern, the cascade transaction, and the public_id-only response construction right here sets the template for the other two slices.
Output: `src/routes/houses/{index,schemas}.ts`, `src/services/house.ts`, `src/test/routes/houses.test.ts`.
Sequence: follows CLAUDE.md structure-first → tests-red → implement (three tasks, three atomic commits).
</objective>
